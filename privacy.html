Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what information WinScout collects and how we use it. WinScout is run by Winning Products, Karachi, Pakistan (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

  • Forms you send. When you join the waitlist or contact us, we get your name, email, market, seller type, topic and message.
  • Saved products and theme. Your saved products and colour theme are kept only in your own browser (local storage). We never receive them.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) may log your IP address and browser type to deliver the site. Product photos are loaded through an image service (images.weserv.nl) and the original shop websites.

We do not use cookies for tracking, we do not run advertising, and we do not sell your data.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To email you about the WinScout Pro launch, if you joined the waitlist.
  • To improve the product list, for example adding niches people ask for.

3. Where it is stored

Form messages pass through Make.com (automation service) and are stored in our Notion workspace. We receive a copy by email. These services keep data on their own secure servers, which may be outside Pakistan.

4. How long we keep it

Waitlist details are kept until Pro launches and for up to 12 months after, unless you ask us to delete them earlier. Contact messages are kept for up to 24 months.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Use the contact form and choose “Delete or update my data”. You can also unsubscribe from any email we send.

6. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, uses a strict content security policy, and never asks for passwords or payment details. Forms have spam protection. No online service is 100% secure, so please do not send sensitive information in messages.

7. Children

WinScout is meant for adults running a business. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact form.
