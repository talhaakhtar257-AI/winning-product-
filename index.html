Method

How we find and score products

The same steps run every morning. No product is added by hand, and every score shows its proof.

1

Search

At 9:00 AM the AI searches Amazon, TikTok Shop, Daraz and trend reports for best sellers in 4 niches, for Global and Pakistan.

2

Score

Each product gets 0–10 on 9 points: demand, profit, competition, solves a problem, happy buyers, price, shipping, all-year sales and safety.

3

Check proof

Scores are capped by proof. No sales numbers means no top score. Repeats from the last 7 days are removed.

4

Publish

At 9:30 AM the best products appear here with photos, prices and supplier links. Each niche refreshes every 3 days.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

Where do the products come from?

Every morning our AI searches public sources — Amazon, TikTok Shop, Daraz and trend articles. It only picks products named in those results, and links the source so you can check.

How often is the list updated?

Every day at 9:30 AM Pakistan time. There are 12 niches in 3 groups, so each niche gets fresh products every 3 days.

Are the cost and sell prices exact?

No. They are AI estimates from public prices. Always open the supplier link and confirm the real cost, shipping and quality before you buy stock.

What does the score mean?

It is a 0–100 mix of 9 points like demand, profit and competition. Scores are capped by proof, so a product without sales numbers cannot be a top winner. See “How we score” above.

What does the Google Trends chart show?

Open any product and press “Show Google Trends chart”. It shows how much people searched for that product on Google in the last 12 months, in Pakistan or worldwide. 100 is the busiest week of the year. A rising line is a good sign.

Is WinScout free?

Yes, everything on this page is free today. A Pro plan with early alerts and full history is planned. Join the waitlist to hear first.

Can I ask for a new niche?

Yes. Send us a message on the contact page with the niche and your market.

Do you keep my data?

Only what you send in a form (name, email and message). Saved products stay in your own browser. Read the privacy policy.
